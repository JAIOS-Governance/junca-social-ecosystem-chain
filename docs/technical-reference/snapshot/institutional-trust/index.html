Internationally informed governance for fair and credible operations

JAIOS Institutional Governance consolidates international legal developments, jurisdictional frameworks, technology and financial conditions, user-protection requirements and global affairs into maintained audit and management standards.

JAIOS Institutional Governanceは、国際法令、各国の制度、技術・金融環境、利用者保護に関する要件および国際情勢を継続的に整理し、監査・管理基準へ反映する機関です。

JUNCA proceeds under JAIOS audit

JUNCA HOLDINGS advances its business, development, operations and public disclosures while subject to JAIOS review and evidence-based assessment.

JUNCA HOLDINGSは、JAIOSによる監査と証拠に基づく評価を受けながら、事業・開発・運用・公開を進めています。

Completion and operation are not represented solely by the assessment of the developing organization. Public information is tied to reviewable records, release controls and verified evidence.

開発主体の判断だけで完成・稼働を公表せず、確認可能な記録、公開統制、検証証跡に基づいて情報を公開します。
